Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

What this app is

Video Fabrikasi is a private tool operated by its owner. It is used only with the owner's own Google accounts, YouTube channels and Instagram professional account (@chloemercer.ai).

Google data we access

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's YouTube channels and to upload files to the owner's Google Drive. It reads only the basic channel information needed to select the correct channel.

Instagram data we access

When the owner connects the Instagram professional account, the app uses the Instagram API to: read the account's basic profile (username and account ID); publish the owner's own photos and videos to that account; and read comments left on the account's own posts so that the owner can review them and, only after the owner approves, post a reply or hide spam. The app does not access other users' profiles, followers, messages or any content outside the owner's own posts.

How Instagram data is used

Comment text and the commenter's public username are shown only to the owner (in a private notification) to decide whether to reply. Comments are not sold, shared, used for advertising or used to train any model. Comment data is kept only as long as needed to handle the reply and is deleted from the owner's computer within 30 days.

How Google data is used

Access is used solely to publish the owner's own videos and to store the owner's own files. The app does not read, analyze, sell, or share any other personal data.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sent to third parties other than Google's and Meta's own APIs.

Revoking access

Google access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. Instagram access can be removed at any time in the Instagram app under Settings > Website permissions > Apps and websites.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data this app holds about you (for example, a comment you left on @chloemercer.ai), email hasanvideofabrikasi@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and your Instagram username. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Video Fabrikasi's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
