Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

What this app is

Video Fabrikasi is a private tool operated by its owner. It is used only with the owner's own Google accounts and YouTube channels.

Google data we access

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's YouTube channels and to upload files to the owner's Google Drive. It reads only the basic channel information needed to select the correct channel.

How the data is used

Access is used solely to publish the owner's own videos and to store the owner's own files. The app does not read, analyze, sell, or share any other personal data.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sent to third parties other than Google's own APIs.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Video Fabrikasi's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
